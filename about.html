About

Imperial MSc Advanced Computing graduate, interested in systems engineering and forward-deployed positions, looking for a role in London, Amsterdam or Prague.

I finished my MSc in Advanced Computing at Imperial in September 2026 and have since co-authored a paper submission to EuroSys '27. I like building systems and finding new ways to solve problems. At Imperial I was introduced to eBPF and DPDK and found them interesting enough to build my thesis around them. AccNet lets an unmodified application use a kernel-bypass network stack: eBPF intercepts its socket system calls and hands the data to a DPDK transport. Building systems and making them work is exactly what I want to keep doing.

Before Imperial I did my BSc in Computer Science at VU Amsterdam, with an exchange semester at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. I spent three summers at MicroStep in Bratislava, the first on data analysis and the next two writing software for CNC machine automation. That meant working with people across several departments, and I liked it. I'm outgoing, and I'd be just as happy embedded in a customer's team, working through problems with them directly.

I'm looking for a graduate or junior role in London, Amsterdam or Prague. I've always liked building systems and seeing them work, so I'm mainly interested in systems engineering roles on networking or infrastructure teams. I'm also outgoing and enjoy working out how to solve new problems, which is why solutions engineering and forward-deployed positions appeal to me as well.

Outside work I play 7-a-side football and have recently taken up tennis. I like organising trips with friends, and I'm planning to start learning a new language next.
